Phase de charge

$$ \frac{du_C}{dt} + \frac{1}{\tau}u_C = \frac{E}{\tau} $$

Phase de décharge

$$ \frac{du_C}{dt} + \frac{1}{\tau}u_C = 0 $$
$$ u_C(t) = U_{C,0} \, e^{-t/\tau} $$
$$ q = C\,u_C \qquad i = \frac{dq}{dt} $$
