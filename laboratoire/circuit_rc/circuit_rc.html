📝 Modèle Mathématique

Phase de Charge

$$ \frac{du_C}{dt} + \frac{1}{\tau}u_C = \frac{E}{\tau} $$
$$ u_C(t) = E(1 - e^{-t/\tau}) $$

Phase de Décharge

$$ \frac{du_C}{dt} + \frac{1}{\tau}u_C = 0 $$
$$ u_C(t) = E \cdot e^{-t/\tau} $$
$$ i = \frac{dq}{dt} \quad \text{et} \quad q = C \times u_C $$
